# Giwu Bible — Launch Video Plan

## What is it
A multi-version Bible reader: read a chapter in one translation (KJV by
default), tap any verse, and see it instantly compared against six other
translations side-by-side. One app, three platforms — Web, Android, Windows.

## Who it's for
Anyone studying or reading Scripture who wants to compare translations
without juggling six tabs or six apps.

## What sets it apart
The tap-to-compare interaction: no searching, no second app, no page reload —
the verse you're already reading becomes a parallel-translation panel in
place. It works the same way on web, native Android and native Windows.

## The hook
Watch a single verse become six.

## Visual identity (pulled straight from the real app, not invented)
- Primary red: `#E30613`, hover `#b0000d`, light tint `#FEF2F2`
- Ink: near-black `#0a0a0a` on white `#ffffff`; dark mode flips to `#0a0a0a` bg / off-white ink
- Scripture text: Georgia serif, 16px, 1.75 line-height
- UI chrome: system sans-serif, small caps/letterspaced labels (11px, 700 weight, 0.08–0.14em tracking) — this is the app's actual label style throughout (sidebar header, panel tabs, footer tagline)
- Logo: red/black cross mark icon (`app-icon.png`), square, rounded corners
- Real footer tagline already in the shipped app: **"BUILT FOR SEEKERS OF TRUTH"** — use verbatim as the punchline, don't invent a new one
- Real hero copy: "Read the King James Version side-by-side with parallel translations. Available on Web, Android, and Windows."
- Real platform badges: Android / Windows / Web (exact icons used in the app)

## Tone
**Polished** — serious, elegant, restrained. This is a Scripture app; the
humor/chaos presets don't fit the subject matter. Confidence through
simplicity, not jokes. Long holds, soft fades, no gimmicks.

## Format
Landscape, 1920×1080, 30fps, ~20s.

## Music
Simple, warm, minimal piano/pad progression at 92 BPM (slow enough to feel
reverent, not sluggish). 4-bar phrase loops under the whole video. Soft UI
click/whoosh sfx locked to the beat grid on every cut and every on-screen
tap, mixed well under the music bed. No percussion hits that feel "launch
hype" — this leans calm-confident, not chaotic.

## Storyboard (target ~20s @ 30fps = 600 frames)

| # | Time | Scene | What's on screen |
|---|------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5s | **Hook** | Black background. The real verse "In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth." types on in white serif, verse number in red. Cuts to red flash wipe. |
| 2 | 2.5–5.5s | **Reveal** | The real Read page (Genesis 1, actual markup/CSS/fonts) scales/fades in full-bleed — sidebar, chapter, verse text, exactly as shipped. |
| 3 | 5.5–11s | **Highlight 1 — Tap to compare** | Simulated click on verse 1. It highlights red. The real parallel panel slides in from the right and the six translation cards (ASV, BBE, Darby, Webster, WEB, YLT) populate one at a time. Caption: "Tap any verse." |
| 4 | 11–15s | **Highlight 2 — Everywhere** | Cut to the real home page hero, then the three real platform badges (Android / Windows / Web) land one by one. Caption: "Web. Android. Windows." |
| 5 | 15–17.5s | **Highlight 3 — Dark mode** | Quick crossfade: the same chapter flips to the app's real dark theme via the settings switch. |
| 6 | 17.5–20s | **Punchline / outro** | Logo mark centers, "Giwu Bible" wordmark, then the real tagline "BUILT FOR SEEKERS OF TRUTH" and `giwu.co.za` fade in, hold, fade to black. |

Every text line holds fully readable for ≥0.3s/word before any cut.
Transitions: scene 1→2 red wipe; 2→3 hard cut on beat; 3→4 soft dip-to-white;
4→5 crossfade (staggered, not a muddy double-exposure — chapter content exits
before dark theme enters); 5→6 fade to black.
